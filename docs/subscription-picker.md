# Guided subscription tracking

Gift lists popular recurring services by category instead of asking people to remember every subscription from scratch. Each selected service is stored as its own bill with the person’s actual amount, next charge date, and billing frequency.

Saving a subscription automatically enables bill reminders and in-app bill notifications. If the person has separately opted into email reminders, the same subscription is also included in email delivery. Gift does not guess prices, connect to cards, or enroll the person with the subscription provider.

The subscriptions screen shows the estimated monthly and annual totals, every active service, its next charge date, and whether reminders are active. People can also add a custom service that is not in the catalog.
